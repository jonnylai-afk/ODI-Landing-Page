A food-industry commercial partner, built on an importer’s experience.

OD International Ltd. is a Canadian food and beverage commercial services company specializing in international trade, strategic sourcing, manufacturer representation, brand management and market development.

Who we are

We work as a commercial intermediary and strategic partner, helping manufacturers, brand principals, importers, distributors and grocery retailers identify and develop profitable business opportunities. Our work goes beyond conventional food brokerage, combining industry knowledge, established commercial relationships, sourcing capability and market-entry services.

We serve North American and international clients, with a particular strength in multi-unit grocery chains, supermarket groups and organized retail networks.

Our history

Olympic Distributors was founded in 1949 and built its business importing Asian and specialty food and selling it wholesale across Canada. Over the decades that meant building supplier relationships across Asia, keeping up with Canadian import rules, and selling to grocery and foodservice buyers across the country.

OD International Ltd. is now the parent company. It leads the representation, sourcing, brand management and market expansion work described on this site, while Olympic Distributors continues as the group’s importer and wholesale distributor.

What we stand for

We aim to build long-term commercial relationships by connecting the right products, brands, suppliers and markets.

Commercial integrity and long-term relationships

We aim for relationships that last well beyond the first order, and we say so when an opportunity isn’t a fit.

Practical industry knowledge

Advice grounded in decades of importing, distributing and selling food.

Data-driven decisions

We quote landed cost, not ex-factory price, and test pricing against shelf and margin data before anyone commits.

Market-specific execution

Each market has its own buyers, rules and economics. Plans are built for the market in front of us.

Cost efficiency and sustainable profitability

Programs have to make money for every party in the chain, at volume and over time.

Confidentiality and professional discretion

Partner, customer and commercial details stay private, and are shared only with qualified parties as a project requires.

Leadership
