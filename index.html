The challenge

Entering a new market typically takes six separate specialists before the first order ships.

Each brings its own brief, its own contract and its own priorities. The client is left to coordinate them, and the gaps between the roles are where launches stall.

Without an integrated partner: six briefs, six contracts

Market researcher

Demand, competitors and pricing

Compliance adviser

Labelling and regulatory readiness

Broker or sales agent

Buyer access and negotiation

Distributor

Channel coverage and fulfilment

Brand manager

Category, promotion and account plans

Logistics coordinator

Freight, customs and delivery

Flow

With OD International: one brief, one plan

One coordinated commercial partner

We coordinate market assessment, sourcing, commercial planning, account development and execution, and engage specialist third parties where they add value.

One brief. One plan. One accountable partner.

How we work

Every engagement starts with a confidential discussion and is agreed in writing before work begins.

  1. 1

    Confidential discussion

    You share your products, markets and objectives. We assess commercial fit and tell you candidly if we are not the right partner.

  2. 2

    Qualification and NDA

    Mutual confidentiality is put in place before any sensitive disclosure. Scope, volumes and resources are confirmed.

  3. 3

    Scoped proposal

    We present the deliverables, milestones, responsibilities and commercial terms for your agreement.

  4. 4

    Execute and report

    We work the plan with buyers, suppliers and partners, and report against the agreed milestones.

Engagement terms are tailored to each mandate and presented at the proposal stage.
