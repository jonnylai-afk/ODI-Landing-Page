Food and beverage brand management services.

Ongoing commercial management for food and beverage brands in North America. We act as your brand and category team, from positioning and pricing to promotions, forecasting and account performance.

Discuss brand growthCall +1 604-420-8022

Who this is for

CEOs and brand directors of food and beverage companies that have distribution, or are about to, and need experienced commercial management without building a full local team.

What we do

Brand development and positioning
Where the brand sits against competitors on benefit, price per unit and shopper use, applied consistently across accounts.
Category and portfolio management
Which products earn their shelf space, which to cut and where the range should grow.
Retail account development
Building distribution and sell-through in multi-unit grocery chains and organized retail.
Pricing architecture and margin
List prices, promotional allowances and retailer margins that hold up at volume.
Promotional planning
Promotional calendars that build volume without eroding margin, with post-event analysis.
Sales forecasting and demand planning
Forecasts that production and logistics can deliver against.
New product launches
Launch timing, sell-in materials and the first months of follow-through.
Performance monitoring
Regular reviews of distribution, velocity, promotions and margin, with clear next steps.

How engagements work

Every engagement is scoped by product, channel and territory, and agreed in writing after an initial assessment. Commercial terms are discussed directly with qualified clients.

Common questions

When does outsourced brand management make sense?

When a brand needs a commercial team in a market before volumes justify its own office, or when an existing team needs category and retail expertise it doesn’t have.

Do you work with brands already in distribution?

Yes. Many engagements start with a review of current distribution, pricing and promotional results, then move to ongoing management.

How do you report?

Through regular reviews against agreed measures: distribution, sales velocity, promotional results and margin.
