Food and beverage go-to-market consulting.

Before you invest in a new market or launch, know whether the numbers work. We assess feasibility, model landed cost and margins, choose the channels and build a launch plan you can carry out.

Request a market consultationCall +1 604-420-8022

Who this is for

Commercial directors and strategy teams at food manufacturers and brand owners preparing to enter Canada or expand across North America, and companies weighing a new product launch.

What we do

Market feasibility assessment
Whether there is room for the product, at what price and against whom.
Competitor and category analysis
Shelf mapping, price per unit, pack formats and the brands you will be compared with.
Market-entry strategy
Which market, channel and partner model to start with, and why.
Commercial pricing models
List price, trade terms and promotional allowances that work at your cost base.
Landed-cost and margin analysis
Factory price to shelf price, with freight, duty, brokerage, distributor and retailer margins built in.
Retail channel selection
Multi-unit grocery chains, Asian and international grocery, independents, wholesale and foodservice compared.
Regulatory and labelling readiness
CFIA requirements, bilingual labelling, the nutrition facts table and front-of-package symbol mapped for specialist review.
Launch planning
Milestones, decision gates, partner needs and the first year’s commercial plan.

How an assessment runs

  1. Scope

    Products, markets, channels and the decisions the work must support.

  2. Research and modelling

    Shelf and competitor research, landed-cost and margin models.

  3. Recommendation

    A written assessment presented to your team.

  4. Launch plan

    Milestones, partners and the first-year plan.

How engagements work

Every engagement is scoped by product, channel and territory, and agreed in writing after an initial assessment. Commercial terms are discussed directly with qualified clients.

Common questions

How long does an assessment take?

It depends on the number of products, markets and channels in scope. We agree the timeline before work starts.

Do you also carry out the launch?

Yes, if you choose. Many clients move from the assessment into representation or brand management with us.

Can you sign off regulatory compliance?

We map the requirements and coordinate with regulatory specialists. Formal compliance sign-off should come from a qualified advisor.
