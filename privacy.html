Privacy notice

Last updated 26 October 2026

Who we are

OD International Ltd. (“we”) operates odinter.com. Our office is at Unit 510 – 6378 Silver Avenue, Burnaby, BC V5H 0J2, Canada. We handle personal information in line with Canada’s Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and British Columbia’s Personal Information Protection Act.

What we collect

When you send an inquiry, we collect what you enter: your name, company, business email, country or region, website, product category and the details of your inquiry. We don’t ask for sensitive personal information, and please don’t include it.

How we use it

  • To respond to your inquiry and assess whether we can help
  • To prepare a proposal and manage any business relationship that follows
  • To keep a record of our correspondence

We don’t sell your information or use it for unrelated marketing. Inquiries are treated as confidential.

Service providers and storage

Website forms are processed by Formspree, Inc., a service provider in the United States, which delivers submissions to our email. Your information may be stored and processed in the United States and may be accessible to authorities there under US law.

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., United States), which may log technical information such as IP addresses for security purposes.

Analytics and cookies

We use Google Analytics 4 (Google LLC, United States) to understand how visitors find and use this website, for example which pages are viewed and whether an inquiry is sent. Google Analytics uses cookies and collects information such as your approximate location, device and browser. It does not give us your name or contact details. You can block these cookies in your browser settings or with Google’s opt-out add-on. We do not use advertising cookies.

How long we keep it

We keep inquiry records for as long as we need them to respond and to manage any resulting business relationship, then delete them.

Your choices

You can ask to see or correct the information we hold about you, or withdraw your consent to our use of it, by emailing info@odinter.com.

Contact

Privacy questions can be sent to info@odinter.com, attention: Privacy Officer, or by mail to the address above.
