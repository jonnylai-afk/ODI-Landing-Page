How engagements work

Every engagement is scoped by product, channel and territory, and agreed in writing after an initial assessment. Commercial terms are discussed directly with qualified clients.

Common questions

Can you source private-label products in Canada?

Yes. We identify Canadian manufacturers and co-packers that produce under the buyer’s brand, and coordinate specifications, packaging and labels.

Will Canadian products meet my market’s requirements?

Not automatically. Canadian origin doesn’t make a product admissible elsewhere; registration, labelling and certificate requirements are set by your market. We confirm them with you and your importer before an order is placed.

What volumes do you work with?

Projects are scoped around regular commercial volumes. Tell us your expected annual quantity and we’ll say whether it fits.

Who handles export documents?

We coordinate the commercial invoice, packing list and certificates with the supplier and your freight forwarder. CFIA export certificates are arranged where the product and market require them.
