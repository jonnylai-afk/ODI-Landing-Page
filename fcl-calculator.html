Assumptions
  • Dimensions and gross weight are per shipping case (master carton). Rows with any blank field are left out of the totals.
  • Volume is carton cube against the container's nominal capacity. Floor-loaded cartons usually reach about 85 to 92% of nominal cube, and palletized loads reach less.
  • Payload is the container's rated limit. Road and rail weight limits on the delivery leg can be lower, so confirm the legal cargo weight with your drayage carrier.
  • Reefer cargo must stay below the load line and leave room for air flow, so usable cube is below the nominal figure.
  • The headline cost per case is the rate divided by total cases. The SKU table splits the same rate by each SKU's share of volume, weight or case count.
  • Container figures are Hapag-Lloyd's published examples for the 20' standard, 40' standard, 40' high cube, 20' reefer and 40' reefer high cube. Boxes vary by manufacturer and carrier.
