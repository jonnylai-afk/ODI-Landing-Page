Factory-direct food sourcing, by the container.

For foodservice groups, food manufacturers and distributors who need a specific product at a better landed cost. We write the specification, find the factory and manage the order through to delivery.

Request a sourcing quoteCall +1 604-420-8022

Who this is for

Restaurant and foodservice groups buying private-label or bulk products, manufacturers buying ingredients, and distributors buying regular volumes. It suits products bought repeatedly in container quantities, including products we don’t stock.

How a sourcing project runs

  1. Specification

    Product, pack size, quality standard, certifications, volume and target landed price.

  2. Factory search and quotes

    A shortlist of suitable factories quoting on the same specification and terms.

  3. Samples

    Samples come to you for approval before anything is ordered.

  4. Proforma and payment

    We issue a proforma invoice; production is scheduled once payment is received.

  5. Production and checks

    We follow production and confirm labels, packing and documents before the goods ship.

  6. Shipping and delivery

    Freight, customs clearance and delivery, or handover at the port if you prefer.

Common questions

Why a two-container minimum?

Factory-direct pricing only pays off at volume. Below two containers, buying from a distributor’s stock is usually cheaper once freight and handling are counted, and the factory needs a run size worth scheduling.

Can you source products you don’t carry?

Yes. Most sourcing projects are for products outside our own range.

Who is the importer of record?

You can import directly, or we can import and deliver to you. We agree this at the quote stage.
